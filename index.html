01Statement

Why the name.

Four words were used to wave away a confirmed kart fault. We built a team around refusing them.

IPR Race Control

Decision of the stewards

Document 01

FromThe Stewards
ToAll drivers
Subject"It's part of racing"
Fact

At a rental karting event in 2026, a kart fault that the organisers themselves had confirmed was dismissed with the words "it's part of racing". No requalification was given. Drivers who had paid and travelled to be there were told to accept it.

Reason

In rental karting the kart isn't yours, the mechanics aren't yours, and none of the preparation is under your control. Giving every driver a fair kart is the organiser's job.

Calling a confirmed fault "part of racing" is an excuse.

Decision

The excuse is not accepted. IPR Racing was founded in June 2026 to race without it.

Excuse rejectedIPR Race Control

02Regulations

Consistency Championship sporting regulations

One flyer lap wins nothing.

Our members live in different cities, so the championship is raced from your own local track. It rewards the hardest skill in a rental kart, which is lapping the same time again and again.

ART. 1

Drive 10 laps

Ten consecutive laps at your local track. Drive more if you like, and your best 10 in a row are scored.

ART. 2

Send proof

Onboard footage, a photo of the timing screen, or a written log taken straight off the display.

ART. 3

Scored on CV

Your coefficient of variation decides the month. Lower is better.

ART. 4

F1-style points

The ten lowest CVs each month score points. Most points from July to December is champion.

ART. 5

Fast and steady

Drive more than 10 laps, and if your fastest lap of the session falls inside your scored 10, you earn a bonus point.

ART. 6

Every lap counts

Fewer than 10 laps in a row costs 1 point per missing lap, if that session is your result for the month.

ART. 7

Ties

A tie in a month goes to the lower standard deviation. A tie for the title goes to the lower average CV, then to more months competed.

ART. 8

Built on trust

No cameras or officials watch our tracks. What you submit is taken as the truth, so be honest with yourself first.

CV=σx̄

Standard deviation of your 10 laps divided by your average lap. Dividing by the average puts a 28-second track and a 55-second track on equal footing, whatever kart you drive.

04Standard

Technical standard for IPR events

The events we're building toward.

The long-term goal is our own karting events, at tracks we choose, run the way every rental event should be. Every IPR event will keep these four promises.

Karts checked before every session

Every kart is checked and verified before it goes out, so no result is decided by a faulty machine.

Equal allocation, no favourites

Who you know has nothing to do with what you drive.

Rules agreed in advance

Agreed by everyone before the day, and never changed to suit anyone once racing starts.

Results earned cleanly

Results mean something because they are earned on track, by the drivers, and nowhere else.
